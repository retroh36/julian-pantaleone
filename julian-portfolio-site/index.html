Featured project

Salon software that takes work off the front desk.

I spent six years at the front desk of a salon and spa with 30,000+ recurring clients. Maestra is the system I wished we had: booking, checkout and team management designed around how a busy desk really works, with a built-in AI assistant that handles scheduling conflicts and inventory behind the scenes.

Your salon’s brain.

Other software keeps your salon’s records. Maestra runs your salon’s day: booking, checkout, team, inventory and owner insights in one calm system, with an AI assistant named Denise working behind the desk.

Open the live demo ↗

Denise, the assistant inside Maestra

“Gil’s running 20 minutes late. I found a fix that only moves one client: Isabella → Bridget at 3:05. Want me to apply it?”

Denise suggests and people decide. Every change is approved and can be undone, and every number she gives comes from the salon’s real data.

The calendar is the heart of the desk. Service colors carry the day; a yellow block means the client is here, a check means they’ve checked out.

Check in, check out

Every screen gets one test: does this take work off the desk, or add to it? A normal visit is two taps. Everything else stays out of the way until it’s needed.

Calm by design

One primary button per screen, status shown as a dot and a word, and color saved for what matters. It’s built for a busy Saturday at the desk.

No AI talks to clients

Clients come for their stylist. Denise works behind the desk, and the guest experience stays with the people they came to see.

About
